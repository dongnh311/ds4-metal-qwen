## Speed

| | Q2_0 | ISTA IQ3_XXS hc-F16 |
|---|---|---|
| prefill t/s | 776 / 835 / 835 | 776 / 801 / 800 |
| decode t/s | 34.22 / 34.74 / 34.73 | 33.46 / 33.64 / 33.54 |
| peak wired, GiB | ~44-45 | ~53-54 |

With MTP (`ds4 --mtp`, 256 tokens, temperature 0). The IQ3_XXS column is the 2026-10-02 overnight warm run.

| prompt | Q2_0 | IQ3_XXS |
|---|---|---|
| prime (code) | 41.35 t/s, 97.2% accepted | 46.17 t/s, 98.0% |
| blue sky (VI) | 37.57 t/s, 71.7% | 41.57 t/s, 80.0% |
| TCP vs UDP | 44.20 t/s, 85.8% | 42.80 t/s, 85.0% |

**Why Q2_0 is not faster here, although ISTA's card shows 93.8 vs 70 t/s:**
- **The card compares a different pair, on another engine.** It compares Q2_0 with IQ2_XS on llama.cpp, where
  lookup-table types decode slowly. In ds4, IQ3_XXS already runs on tuned paths:
  - prefill dequantizes into the tensor-op tiles;
  - decode uses `gsq_mv`;
  - 9 IQ2_XXS layers use the M5 kernels.
- **Q2_0 cuts few bytes per token.** A decoded token reads 3951 MiB of weights on Q2_0 against 4572 MiB on
  IQ3_XXS (−13.6%; `sp3/bytes_per_token.py`).
  - 84% of those bytes are dense tensors: attention, GDN, the 1.2 GB F16 hc mixers, the router.
  - The routed experts, where Q2_0 saves the most, are only 16%: 10 of 512 experts per layer.
  - The 8.8 GiB smaller file is mostly experts a token does not read.
- **Decode is largely GPU-compute bound** (`2026-10-02-ista-vs-prod-decode-profile.md`), so 13.6% fewer bytes
  give +3.5%.
- **Ivan's MTP head accepts fewer drafts on Vietnamese** on the Q2_0 trunk (71.7% against 80.0%).
